Scene title for context only: La sortie derrière soi / The exit behind.
A spatial architectural metaphor for the story's ending, built as a HUMAN opaque concrete survey maze, not a rendering of the original invisible alien walls. Camera at human eye height looking BACK over the route just traveled. An enormous gray rectangular blind wall dominates the center-right at close range, forming the end of a long narrow ochre-floored passage. A thin parallel wall offsets in front of it, making the obvious forward route appear closed. At the very near left edge, partially hidden behind the end of a thick foreground rectangular buttress, a simple narrow UNFRAMED gap between wall blocks opens directly onto a vast bright empty yellow-green horizon and the open ochre plain. That opening was immediately behind the traveler, mere steps away; it is visible to the viewer without arrows or explanation. A low white rectangular survey marker beyond the gap echoes a second marker deep down the dead-end passage, creating mistaken alignment. No stairs, bridge, tunnel, overhead roof or monumental gate. The center and right two-thirds are cool dark interlocking planar walls; the left third has a radiant plain and pale yellow sky sliver, olive above. Include a low foreground block bench with one blank flat rectangular record tablet, suggesting an exhausted pause without showing a person, corpse, helmet or text. Strong intimate asymmetry, relief and tragic near-access, geometry around 15–25 boxes, convincing warm light bouncing through the hidden side opening, subtle white camera fill in the dead end. All walls matte gray, crisp large simple planes.
