Scene title for context only: La veille des lumières / The vigil of lights.
Deep Venusian twilight on an open ochre plateau. View from seated human height near the center of a huge EMPTY space encircled in the distance by twelve lonely squat square gray beacon pillars, each topped by a small rectangular softly luminous pale white panel. This is an architectural reinterpretation of a ring of watching torches: no actual people or creatures. Show a wide near-semicircle of this ring and its distant continuation; spacing is quietly irregular, most lights small in the image. Center remains open and visually traversable, without a single visible wall. Two short dark heavy rectangular equipment boxes sit close at bottom left, one lid open represented by one rotated flat rectangle; a small flat metal record tablet rests beside them, completely blank. A tiny faceted crystal lies on a low dark block near the boxes and catches a restrained white glimmer, secondary to the watchful distant lights. Beyond the ring, sparse low square dark silhouettes barely interrupt the horizon. The upper sky is deep desaturated olive, a narrow pale sulfur-yellow band persists at the horizon, unmistakably the series' yellow-green sky, not blue or orange. Soft small light pools at each beacon and ochre bounce near camera, no bloom, rays, haze or star field. Foreground shadows deep but shapes readable. Emphasize loneliness, silent attention and ambiguous protection rather than hostile monsters. Around 25–35 boxes plus tiny crystal prefab; entirely still simple geometry.
