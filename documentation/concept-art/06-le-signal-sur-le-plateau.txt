Scene title for context only: Le signal sur le plateau / The signal on the plateau.
At the crest of a broad stark quarry plateau, the viewer discovers a single small brilliant white faceted crystal on a low square gray plinth far across a completely open ochre plain. It is the visual destination, right of center, small but unmistakable. The entire scene is about seductive distance and apparent accessibility. In the immediate foreground, two enormous offset solid rectangular rock-gray blocks form a narrow dark slit framing the view, left block high and right block very low, asymmetrical. Foreground stepped ledge leads down onto the plain with only three broad simple box steps. Far horizon has three low broken rectangular quarry terraces and one thin vertical survey monolith, no skyline of buildings. The sky occupies half the open view, yellow at the low horizon and green above. The crystal is a small group of three pointed faceted transparent prismatic forms on a heavy low square support, with bounded white glow, no rays or halo. Camera human eye level from inside the shaded crest opening, wide oblique view. Soft bright plain against cool gray foreground occlusion. Very sparse 15–25 major boxes plus the crystal prefab, no path or visible barrier between viewer and crystal. No body or abandoned suit.
