Beyond the quarry blocks

A marker is visible among the quarry volumes. The way back remains open.

Brief de composition :
A colossal abandoned extraction block fills most of the view, yet its base opens through one small entry at human scale. Beyond that entry the interior must read as a genuine empty void, not a painted face: the mass spans overhead while the opening remains narrow and deep. Keep the surrounding site bare and monumental. Place one box named "portal" inside the visible opening so it reads as a fixed datum against the dark emptiness.
